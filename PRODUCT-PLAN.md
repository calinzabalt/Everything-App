## Product

A dashboard for freelance work: jobs, leads, and later clients.

Left: menu. Right: the selected view.

| Menu | What it shows |
|------|----------------|
| Job Finder | Saved jobs |
| Lead Finder | Saved leads |
| Clients | Clients and revenue (later) |

Grok bot agents bring jobs and leads. The app saves them and shows them in the dashboard.

---

## Audience

Freelance developer. WordPress, React, TypeScript. Mid and senior. Romania first, then UK and USA.

---

## Job Finder (first)

Menu: **Job Finder**.

- List of all saved jobs
- Add a job by hand
- Add a job via API (for Grok bot)

Each job: Check job and Apply (opens the original listing). No automatic apply.

Filters later: skills, level, country.

---

## Lead Finder (second)

Same pattern as Job Finder.

- List of all saved leads
- Add a lead by hand
- Add a lead via API (for Grok bot)

Outreach, follow-up, and status come after the list works.

---

## Clients (last)

Client status (active, not active, in progress) and revenue (weekly, monthly, yearly; project, extras, maintenance). Same dashboard.
